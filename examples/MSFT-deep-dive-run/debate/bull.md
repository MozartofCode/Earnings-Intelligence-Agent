# Bull case · MSFT · 2026-11-18
Price: $508.29 (close 2026-11-18) [D6]

## Thesis
The market treats AI capex as a permanent cash drag, but it already covers its depreciation. If revenue holds about 19–20% growth and operating margin stays at or above 46% through FY27 (to 2027-06-30) while capex intensity peaks, a normalized post-SBC FCF margin of about 27% is reachable. Value if that holds: about $605 (+19%). Disproven if FY27 Q2 or Q3 shows Azure growth below 45% cc or operating margin below 45%.

## Variant perception / What the market is missing
- **What consensus believes:** FY27 Q2 revenue of $96.6–98.5B (+19–21%) [D12]. The price implies a 13.4% ten-year revenue CAGR at a 25% margin, or 17.8% year-1 growth on a 15-year fade [V2].
- **Tone:** a $1.3B beat over the top of guidance drew a -0.22% move [E4][D8]; the stock is 8.5% below its 52-week high [D6].
- **The anchor (judgment):** trailing cash. TTM post-SBC FCF is $47.4B, a 13.7% margin [V1], and only about $12.7B (est.) fully loaded [F-T1]. That is a peak-investment snapshot; the trusted income statement (accruals -7.1% [D7]) shows the capex earning.

## Pillars
**1. Demand exceeds supply, so guidance is a floor.**
- *Evidence:* revenue beat the top of guidance in 4 of 4 quarters, and Azure beat its cc guide by 1–4 pts each time. Azure is guided to 49–50% for FY27 Q2, up from 48% [E-T2][E7]. Constant-currency growth went from 15% to 19% [E-T1]. RPO stands at $677B (+73%) [D2]. Management: demand "continues to exceed supply" [E4]; new capacity is monetized on arrival (secondary) [BU3].
- *What has to be true:* the FY27 Q1 capex shortfall ($44.6B vs a guide of "over $50B", blamed on delayed third-party capacity) delays revenue rather than signalling weaker demand [E-F7].
- *How we'll know:* FY27 Q2 revenue at or above the $96.55B midpoint and Azure at or above 49% cc, around 2027-01-27 (est.) [D11].

**2. Operating leverage shows the capex is paying back.**
- *Evidence:* FY27 Q1 operating margin was 49.8%, up from 48.9% a year earlier, even as quarterly depreciation rose to $11.1B from $7.1B [D5][D1]. Incremental margin was 54.5%. [E-F2].
- *What has to be true:* margin holds as depreciation builds. The Q2 guide implies about 46.8% (est.) [E-T3].
- *How we'll know:* operating margin at or above 46% in FY27 Q2 and Q3 (late Jan and late Apr 2027).

**3. Capex intensity can bend, because much of it is short-lived.**
- *Evidence:* the CFO said capex has pivoted to "short-lived assets" (CPUs and GPUs) that can be slowed if demand changes [E4]. Calendar-2026 capex guidance has come down from ~$190B to ~$175B to ~$170B while revenue guidance rose [E8].
- *What has to be true:* the $35.7B of component receivables unwinds into delivered servers, a timing item in forensics' judgment [F-F2]. Capex plus lease additions also need to fall from ~45% of revenue toward the mid-20s by FY29 (judgment).
- *How we'll know:* capex flat to down q/q in FY27 Q2, and component receivables at or below ~$45B in the Q2 10-Q.
- *Weakness:* no quarter yet shows intensity falling; this is a forecast.

**4. A switching-cost franchise with pricing power.**
- *Evidence:* M365 commercial cloud revenue grew 17% on 5% seat growth [I2]. Copilot has over 30M paid seats, about 6.7% penetration (est.) [I14]. The Agents and Infra segment margin is 52.9% [I3].
- *How we'll know:* M365 commercial cloud at or above +15% in FY27 Q2; a returning Copilot seat count (omitted in FY27 Q1) [BU2].

## Steelman of the bear
**Their best argument:** a 47–50% margin is a depreciation-lag artifact. FY26 capacity spend was about $160B (est.) against $38.5B of D&A. Another $364.6B of leases has not yet commenced, and datacenter lives were extended from 15 to 25 years [F-F1][F-F3][E-F7]. Infrastructure is commoditizing and OpenAI (10.8% of revenue) is non-exclusive [I2][I12]. On this view, true FCF (~$12.7B TTM, est.) never normalizes, and the 31% break-even margin exceeds anything Microsoft has delivered [V3].

**My answer is partial.** Margin *expanded* while depreciation rose 56% y/y [D1][D5]. Capex guidance is falling as revenue guidance rises [E8]. My case needs 27%, not 31%, but only because I assume a longer runway. **I cannot answer the lease wave.** $364.6B of commitments through FY2034 will add amortization that today's margin does not reflect, and the useful-life change flatters FY27. If intensity has not fallen by the FY28 capex framework (July 2027), the bear is right.

## What would make me abandon this case
1. FY27 Q2 revenue below the $96.0B low end, or Azure below 45% cc.
2. Operating margin below 45% in FY27 Q2 or Q3 as depreciation rises.
3. Component receivables above $45B without capex absorbing them, a cut in OpenAI commitments, or RPO below $625B.

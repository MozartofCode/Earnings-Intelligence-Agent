## The short version

The filings confirm the report's checkable facts about **related parties** and **rehiring**. The $983M paid to Ablecom and Compuware matches the 10-K tables to the million, the Special Committee confirmed nine rehires, and Leadtek became a newly disclosed related party. Later events back the broader concern about **controls and governance**: EY resigned, saying it could no longer rely on management's representations, and material weaknesses persisted through June 2026. The headline **accounting manipulation** and **Russia sanctions-evasion** claims have not been established by any filing, regulator or court. There was no restatement, BDO gave a clean opinion on the FY24 statements, and the company's review found no misconduct. A March 2026 indictment of ex-director Wally Liaw concerns alleged diversion to **China**, not Russia, and is a charge, not a conviction. DOJ, SEC and BIS inquiries remain open.

## The claims that matter most

- **#2-3 Revenue manipulation and channel stuffing: not verifiable**: **For:** the 2020 SEC case shows a history (SEC Rel. 2020-190). EY raised concerns about governance and internal control in July 2024 and resigned in October (accn 0001375365-24-000036). **Against:** the Special Committee "did not disagree with any of the Company's revenue recognition conclusions" across 52 deals (accn 0001375365-24-000044). BDO's opinion says the FY24 statements "present fairly, in all material respects", and nothing was restated (accn 0001375365-25-000004). The committee is the company's own review and its scope was limited. DOJ and SEC subpoenas are disclosed with no charges (accn 0001375365-25-000027).
- **#15-16 Russia exports and shell companies: not verifiable**: The 10-K says the company "had last recorded revenue from Russia in February 2022" (accn 0001375365-25-000004). That statement and the report's third-party trade data can both be true. The Special Committee "did not identify products that were sold to Russian customers or shipped to Russia in violation of" sanctions in force (accn 0001375365-24-000044). No regulator action on Russia appears in filings through the FY26 10-K.
- **#19 Export-control compliance: partly supported**: In March 2026, SDNY indicted Wally Liaw (SVP and director, one of the 2018 departures later rehired), a Taiwan sales manager and a contractor. They are charged with an alleged export-control conspiracy routing servers to China via brokers (8-K accn 0001375365-26-000011; FY26 10-K accn 0001375365-26-000022). Press reports put the alleged diversion at ~$2.5B (The Register, 2026-03-20). The company is not a defendant. Its Munger Tolles/AlixPartners probe found no evidence that current senior management knew. These are charges, not findings of guilt, and they concern China, not the Russia flows in the report.
- **#18 Controls and governance: supported (medium)**: EY: "we are resigning due to information that has recently come to our attention which has led us to no longer be able to rely on management's and the Audit Committee's representations" (accn 0001375365-24-000036).

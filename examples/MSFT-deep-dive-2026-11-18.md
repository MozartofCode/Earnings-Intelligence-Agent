## The short version

**Research view: Unattractive, medium conviction.** Desk view: Microsoft's operating engine is accelerating (constant-currency growth 15% to 19%, Azure +48%, revenue above the top of guidance 4 of 4 times), but at $508.29 the price already asks for a margin MSFT has never earned after SBC: the 10-year break-even FCF margin is 31.1% vs a best of 29.1%. The view hinges on (1) whether AI demand outside OpenAI keeps growing now that OpenAI (10.8% of revenue, at least ~32% of RPO, est.) is non-exclusive, and (2) where FCF margin settles once ~$160B a year of capacity spend and $364.6B of uncommenced leases flow through. The probability-weighted value is $399 (-21%), and the upside/downside ratio is 0.46. **Strongest argument against:** on a 15-year fade at a 25% margin, the price needs only today's ~18% growth, and at a 7.5% WACC the base case is worth $512. The verdict is a bet that ten years, not fifteen, is the right horizon for a build-out this capital-intensive.

## Business & moat

- **Moat: Wide · Stable (two businesses, two trends)**: Primary power is switching costs: M365 commercial cloud +17% on +5% seats, with Copilot at ~6.7% of seats (est.) [I2][I14]. AI infrastructure is commoditizing: Microsoft's cloud share is 19% vs 20% a year earlier (Synergy, est.) [I11].
- **OpenAI went from cornered resource to non-exclusive customer**: OpenAI supplied $10.0B, or 10.8%, of FY27 Q1 revenue. Since the 2026-04-27 amendment (secondary reporting) it can buy compute from any cloud, with reported commitments of ~$300B at Oracle and ~$138B at AWS [I2][I12][I17].

The profit pool is Agents and Infra: $40.6B of operating income on $76.8B of revenue (52.9% margin), 88% of segment operating income. Devices and Consumer earned $5.4B on $15.5B, with revenue down 3% [I3]. The FY27 recast puts M365 (the moat) and Azure (the capital-intensive part) in the same segment, so the 52.9% does not show what AI infrastructure earns on its own [I3]. Desk view: industry structure is **Deteriorating**. Buyers are concentrated in a few AI labs (Oracle's $664B RPO sits on the same labs as Microsoft's $677B) and suppliers are strong [I8][I2].

## Financial quality

Margins and returns are excellent, but cash conversion is falling fast. Operating margin reached 47.1% TTM and ROIC 28.8% (FY26), yet FCF/NI fell from 0.90 (FY22) to 0.50 (FY26) as capex rose to $135.9B TTM, 39% of revenue [D5][D7][V7]. Post-SBC FCF margin fell from 25.8% (FY24) to 16.4% (FY26) and 13.7% TTM [V1][V7]. Reported FCF also excludes $24.6B of FY26 finance-lease additions and a $19.9B component-procurement outflow. On that basis FY26 FCF was ~$22.5B (est.) vs $67.0B reported [F-T2]. Net cash is $29.0B, but finance-lease liabilities are $71.0B and another $364.6B of leases has been signed but not yet commenced [D5][V5][F-F3].

## Earnings trajectory

- **Guidance credibility: High**: Revenue landed above the top of the guided range 4 of 4 times, and Azure beat its constant-currency guide by 1-4 pts each time. FY27 Q2 is guided to $96.0-97.1B with Azure at 49-50% (secondary source) [E-T2][E7][E8].
- **Commercial RPO flat for three quarters; bookings -12%**: RPO went $625B, $627B, $678B, $677B. Ex-OpenAI RPO (+25% in FY26 Q4) was not given for FY27 Q1. Management warns of "material volatility in bookings" [E2][E1][E4][D2][E7].

Operating earnings are accelerating. Constant-currency revenue growth rose from 15% to 19% over three quarters, incremental operating margins run ~50%, and FY27 Q1 operating margin was 49.8% [E-T1][E-F2]. Two caveats. Q1 is seasonally the low-opex quarter: the Q2 guide implies ~46.8% (est.) vs 47.1% a year ago [E-T3]. And non-GAAP EPS growth slowed to +18% from +21-24% [E-F5]. GAAP EPS is distorted by OpenAI and Anthropic marks, so the operating line is the cleaner gauge [D-log 8]. The next print is ~2027-01-27 (est., unconfirmed) [D11].

## Forensic check

- **Material: fully loaded FCF ~$22.5B in FY26 vs $67.0B reported (est.)**: $24.6B of finance-lease additions and a $19.9B component-procurement outflow sit outside capex. TTM is ~$12.7B (est.), or $39.3B if the component outflow is a timing item [F-F1][F-T2].
- **Material: component receivables $8.2B to $35.7B in five quarters**: "Other receivables related to activities to facilitate the purchase of server components", plus $12.6B of restricted supplier investments. Counterparty not disclosed [F-F2].
- **Material: $364.6B of leases not yet commenced (77% of equity)**: Commencing through FY2034; finance-lease liabilities are already $71.0B vs $35.5B of debt [F-F3].
- **Material, disclosed: OpenAI is investee, top customer and debtor**: $10.0B of FY27 Q1 revenue (10.8%), $7.2B receivable, ~24% stake under HLBV; FY26 dilution gain $6.5B [F-F4].
- **Clean: accruals, receivables, audit**: Receivables +15.7% vs revenue +17.8%; DSO 81-91 days for 5 years; unqualified Deloitte opinion, effective controls. Server lives unchanged; datacenter and office-building lives were extended 15 to 25 years in FY27, with "minimal" FY27 operating-income benefit per the CFO [F-F6][E4].

## Valuation

At $508.29 the market prices a normalized business, not today's cash. At the TTM 13.7% post-SBC margin, the price needs a 22.0% ten-year revenue CAGR. At a 25% normalized margin it needs 13.4% (8.5% WACC), close to the 14.6% five-year record but on a base twice as large [V1][V2][V7]. Desk view: the hinge is normalized FCF margin. At 19% year-1 growth the 10-year break-even margin is 31.1%, above any post-SBC margin in the FY22-26 data (best 29.1%) and before ~6% of revenue in finance-lease additions [V3][V5].

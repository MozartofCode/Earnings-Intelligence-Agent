# Disclaimer

earnings-desk is an open-source research tool. **It is not investment advice**, and nothing it produces is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security.

- Reports are generated by AI from public data (SEC EDGAR filings, optional third-party APIs and web search). Data can be incomplete, delayed, mis-tagged or simply wrong, and AI analysis can misread it.
- Every report cites its sources so you can check them. **Verify anything important against the original filing before acting on it.**
- Scores such as Beneish M, Altman Z and Piotroski F are screening heuristics with known false positives. Valuation outputs depend entirely on their assumptions.
- The authors and contributors accept no liability for decisions made using this software. Consult a licensed financial professional for advice suited to your situation.

SEC EDGAR data is public. Use of third-party APIs (Financial Modeling Prep, Finnhub, Alpha Vantage, Yahoo Finance) is subject to those providers' terms.
